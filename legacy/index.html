How we work

From first pulse to production.

01
Week 0–1

Discovery & Blueprint

Workshops, technical audit and a fixed-scope blueprint. You know exactly what will be built, by whom and by when — before anything is signed.

02
Week 1–2

Design & Architecture

Interface design and system architecture run in parallel — clickable prototypes on one track, schema and infrastructure decisions on the other.

03
Weekly sprints

Build in Sprints

Shippable increments every week with demo videos, staging links and honest changelogs. You watch the product form in real time.

04
Launch window

Harden & Launch

Load testing, security review, monitoring and rollout plans. Launch day is a checklist, not a gamble.

05
Ongoing

Run & Evolve

Post-launch, we operate and iterate — performance budgets, feature velocity and quarterly technical roadmaps.
